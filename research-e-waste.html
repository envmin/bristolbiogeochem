E-waste bioleaching using acidophilic bacteria

Acid-loving bacteria that recover valuable metals from old electronics. Part of BioXtract.

What we are doing

Bioleaching uses microbes to dissolve metals out of waste so they can be recovered. We use acidophilic bacteria, which thrive in very acidic conditions, to leach metals from e-waste. This project is part of BioXtract.

Why it matters

Electronic waste, such as old circuit boards, contains high concentrations of valuable metals including gold, copper and rare earth elements. Bioleaching offers a low-carbon alternative to conventional chemical and thermal recovery.

Find out more about BioXtract.

How we approach it

We grow acidophilic bacteria in the lab and expose them to electronic waste. We measure which metals are released into solution, how quickly, and under which conditions, so the process can be compared with conventional recovery methods.

Questions we are asking

  • Which metals can bacteria release from e-waste, and how selectively?
  • How can bioleaching be made fast enough to compete with chemical recovery?
  • Once metals are in solution, what is the best way to recover them in a usable form?
