Environmental Mineralogy

Led by Dr James M. Byrne · University of Bristol

About Us

The Environmental Mineralogy Group in the School of Earth Sciences at the University of Bristol investigates natural processes involving iron minerals, with a strong focus on microbe–mineral interactions and biomineralisation. Our research aims to advance understanding of environmentally and societally important processes.

By combining laboratory experiments with advanced spectroscopic and microscopic techniques, we reveal how bacteria produce minerals — and how these biogenic materials can be harnessed for metal recovery, water remediation, and low‑cost energy applications.
