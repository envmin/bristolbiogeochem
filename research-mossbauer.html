57Fe Mössbauer spectroscopy and data analysis

New ways to analyse Mössbauer data, through our web-based platform MinSight.

What we are doing

Mössbauer spectroscopy uses gamma rays to probe iron atoms in terms of oxidation state and mineral composition. We are developing new methods for analysing the data from this technique through a web-based platform.

Why it matters

The platform compares users' own data with a growing database, so that both simple and complex samples can be analysed and interpreted quickly.

Try the platform at MinSight. We also offer 57Fe Mössbauer measurements to industry and academia through our analytical services.

How we approach it

We measure iron-bearing samples across a wide range of temperatures, from 4.2 K up to room temperature, because many iron minerals only show their distinctive signals when cold. We build these measurements and published spectra into the MinSight database, and develop fitting tools that help users compare their own data against it.

Questions we are asking

  • How can closely related iron minerals, especially very small or poorly crystalline ones, be told apart with confidence?
  • How can published Mössbauer data be gathered in a form that others can search and reuse?
  • Which parts of fitting and interpretation can be made faster and more consistent between laboratories?
